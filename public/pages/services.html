About our Services

How can we help you?

With leading expertise in cryptographic technologies, multi-application smart-cards, trusted-environments, strong authentication systems and digital signature-based authorization, we offer a range of services to help you conceive, design, implement, validate and deploy your secure solution.

We help our customers innovate securely, as may be seen by our project portfolio, aiding them to developing secure solutions without compromising their business needs.

Technical Consulting

Nascent offers technical consulting services, using our experience in leading-edge projects to aid you in choosing the right cryptographic technologies, and to put them to use effectively and securely.

Mentoring

Nascent's experienced staff can act as mentors to support your team through the entire process of designing and developing software and solutions based on modern cryptographic technologies, trusted hardware, applications and environments.

With our assistance, your team can stay focused on product development. The mentoring process allows shortening the learning-curve of the advanced technologies we have spent years understanding and working with.

Our know-how reduces your time to market and deployment, while our know-how-not-to reduces your risk and TCO by eliminating the trial-and-error or copycat approaches so often used by inexperienced developers.

Development

Nascent can develop proofs-of-concept, reference implementations or real-world robust secure solutions, applications, software components or libraries.

Code and Design Reviews

Secure project design and cryptographic application coding can be difficult and error-prone. Nascent can assist you in validating and improving your project designs and specifications, as well as performing secure code reviews where necessary.

Technology Evaluation

Over the years, Nascent has been called upon to do technology evaluations for a number of companies.
